# Research policy

Read _library/research-needed.json to find categories without reviewed examples.
Use official documentation or the original tutorial publisher. Verify the page
before recording findings through references:source --file SOURCE.json.
Keep URLs, publisher, checked date, categories, findings, implementation ideas
and limits. Do not download or reuse source art or logos automatically.

Research records are design guidance, not verified local video examples.
Empty local categories remain empty even when online research is available.
The planner retains existing original presets when no confident local match
exists. Codex can consult researched sources for a requested missing technique.
Do not claim that a runtime process browsed the internet when Codex performed
the search, and never imply an unimplemented AI vision service is installed.
