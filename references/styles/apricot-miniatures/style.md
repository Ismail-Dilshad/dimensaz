# Apricot Miniatures

Reusable reference style. Folder `references/styles/apricot-miniatures`. Aliases: `style-4`.

**Feel.** A warm, human-scale first step. Small figures and tactile controls make action feel approachable.

**Picture.** Cream #FAF5EC, peach #FFD3B3, orange #FF6A1A, espresso #1A1411 and sand #E2D2B8. A lightly dotted cream ground supports sculpted ivory steps, a tiny shop or a rounded orange button. Use dark, rounded RTL headlines and espresso lettering on orange emphasis blocks for readability.

**Movement.** Only posters document this family. Proposed action: hold the button base still while the fingertip depresses the cap over 0.45–0.55 s. Cut on its settled circular shape to the pin in the next section. Keep the dots and headline fixed.

**Use for.** Welcoming first-time customers, explaining a practical next step, or encouraging someone who feels stuck. Alias style-4.



## Layered scene direction

Bring a cream fingertip to an orange cap on a stationary pedestal. Match miniature scale, cream dot field, warm shadows and dark type. Assign actual separate assets and a timed action to the moving part. Cite a poster or the Classic source in the scene plan.

## Observed look (style-4)

- **Palette:** cream, espresso and vivid orange
- **Background:** warm dot grid, faint diagonals and peach corners
- **Materials:** cream clay, orange gloss, expressive figure and worn wood
- **Lighting:** warm upper-left light and long soft brown shadows
- **Typography:** espresso headlines; source white-on-orange needs contrast correction
- **Meaning:** action, waiting and visibility
- **Composition:** Dominant physical mechanism, variable top/bottom/side text, deliberate overlap and breathing room; at most two supporting accents.
