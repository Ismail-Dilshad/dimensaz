# Emerald Contrast

Reusable reference style. Folder `references/styles/emerald-contrast`. Aliases: `style-5`.

**Feel.** A measured comparison in which two objects make a distinction easier to understand.

**Picture.** Forest #06201A, secondary green #0B5C45, dark grid #0F3A30, lime #C6F432 and pearl #F4FFF0. Use a deep green studio floor, pearl ceramic or pale metal, and one lime detail. In the posters the sphere/pyramid and eye/bag scale carry the comparison. Use pearl lettering, with forest type on a lime phrase band.

**Movement.** No clips or written motion accompany the numbered folder. Proposed action: settle one pan of the scale over 0.5–0.6 s while the fulcrum, backdrop and copy stay still. Hold both sides long enough to compare them. Use the fulcrum as the handoff point.

**Use for.** Evaluating alternatives, explaining value, or helping a careful buyer weigh a decision. Alias style-5.



## Layered scene direction

Use separate pans and payloads to compare a pearl object against a lime one. Fixed fulcrum, common light and perspective, deep green ground. Assign actual separate assets and a timed action to the moving part. Cite a poster or the Classic source in the scene plan.

## Observed look (style-5)

- **Palette:** deep emerald, ivory and electric lime
- **Background:** dark-green square grid, sparse lines and organic corners
- **Materials:** green metal, pearl objects and lime glow
- **Lighting:** cinematic green floor light and lime rims
- **Typography:** ivory headlines; dark-green lettering inside lime emphasis
- **Meaning:** comparison, conversion, tailored help
- **Composition:** Dominant physical mechanism, variable top/bottom/side text, deliberate overlap and breathing room; at most two supporting accents.
