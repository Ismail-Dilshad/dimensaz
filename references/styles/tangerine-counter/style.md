# Tangerine Counter

Reusable reference style. Folder `references/styles/tangerine-counter`. Aliases: `styles-with-video-3` (filmed tangerine staging).

**Feel.** A friendly storefront offer with a strong graphic silhouette and warm paper beneath it.

**Picture.** Working swatches are cream #FBF2DF, tangerine #FF651E, indigo #251D56, muted lavender #BDB5CB and paper shadow #D7C5A9. Use a faint cream grid, an occasional folded edge, a chunky molded sale tag or striped storefront and a grounded shadow. Pair bold indigo RTL type with an orange highlight slab.

**Movement.** The sale tag pivots edge-on and back, the shop front rises, the phone straightens and sound rings change size. Several samples hold the same composition for most of the clip. Use a single 0.45–0.55 s pivot that stops face-on, keep the headline still, then match the face of the tag to a card.

**Use for.** A local offer, a retail event or a short explainer for price-conscious shoppers. Alias styles-with-video-3.



## Layered scene direction

A striped shop rises into place, then its separate orange sale tag pivots face-on. Cream paper, indigo structure, chunky orange molding and grounded shadows. Assign actual separate assets and a timed action to the moving part. Cite a poster or the Classic source in the scene plan.
