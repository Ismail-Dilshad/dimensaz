# Classic

Reusable reference style. Folder `references/styles/classic`. Aliases: none.

**Feel.** A clear, friendly narrated explanation with space between the spoken thought and the object illustrating it.

**Picture.** White #FFFFFF, cool mist #F3F6FC, pale grid #D5E0F7, cobalt #1F5FFF and navy #0B1B3A. Keep the square grid, pale blue corner wash and small glossy 3D objects with cool contact shadows. Dark RTL copy and white type on a blue chip carry the emphasis. Keep the chip attached to the meaningful phrase.

**Movement.** In the actual export, the cart and shop trade prominence, words accumulate with the voice, the megaphone sends rings, then a paper plane leaves the phone. For the improved Classic, use a 0.45–0.55 s slide to a shared anchor, reveal the intact phrase on its first spoken cue, and hold. Give each section a new symbol. Show visible content whenever a device enters.

**Use for.** A shop owner explaining a service, a narrated FAQ, or a local business seeking a calm, familiar introduction. Explicit “Classic” always selects this card. The existing working render is immediately available through the card's original project. The improved demonstration belongs to Phase 3.



## Layered scene direction

Keep a blue shop steady as a loaded cart settles beside it. Later place a real message layer on the phone. Glossy cobalt and porcelain, regular grid, distinct blue phrase chips and clear narration space. Assign actual separate assets and a timed action to the moving part. Cite a poster or the Classic source in the scene plan.
