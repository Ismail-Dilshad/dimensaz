# Music library

Tracks here are used as background music for videos of their mood. Add them with
`pnpm tempoloom music add FILE --mood calm --license cc0` (only music you may use: CC0, public
domain, CC BY with a credit, your own, or licensed to you); `index.json` records each track's
moods, tempo, beat and license. When no track fits a video's mood, Tempoloom composes an original
piece for that video instead. See docs/guides/music.md.
